DECEMBER REACH / DESKTOP ENVIRONMENT

auroraADE

Aesthetic Desktop Environment

auroraADE is the desktop environment direction within reachOS. Its target is x86_64, with a focus on the desktop layer rather than the whole operating-system platform.

Explore the architecture

View the reachOS source

A desktop environment has its own place in the system.

auroraADE is the name for the desktop-facing environment direction. reachOS is the wider operating-system project, while Fabric Dessert is the virtual hardware platform described in the reachOS source.

Keeping these responsibilities separate helps distinguish the desktop experience from the lower-level platform that hosts it.
