A desktop environment for reachOS

Meet auroraADE.

A desktop environment with its own direction.

Explore auroraADE Explore reachOS ↗

A desktop environment within a bigger system.

reachOS is the operating-system project. auroraADE is its desktop environment direction, with a distinct role and visual identity.

This page introduces auroraADE specifically; it does not present auroraADE as a separate operating system or claim that the illustrated desktop is a released product.

The project direction

Three parts of the auroraADE identity.

Desktop-focused

auroraADE is the desktop environment within the reachOS project, focused on the desktop experience.

x86_64 direction

The planned architecture direction for auroraADE is x86_64, distinct from the overall reachOS project.

Aesthetic by design

The name expands to Aesthetic Desktop Environment: a desktop experience with its own identity.

Part of reachOS

auroraADE belongs to the wider reachOS project; it is not a replacement name for the operating system.

One project. Distinct layers.

auroraADE is the desktop-environment direction within reachOS, while reachOS remains the broader operating-system project.
