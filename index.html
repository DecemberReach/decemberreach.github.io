REACHOS / DESKTOP ENVIRONMENT

Meet auroraADE.

A desktop environment for reachOS.

auroraADE is the Aesthetic Desktop Environment direction within reachOS, with its own identity and a focus on the desktop experience.

Discover auroraADE Explore reachOS

The desktop is one layer of the system.

reachOS is the operating-system project. auroraADE is its desktop environment direction.

That separation keeps the roles clear: reachOS names the wider project, while auroraADE names the desktop environment within it.
